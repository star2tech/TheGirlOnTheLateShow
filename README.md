# The Girl On the Late Night Show

## **What this story is about:**
 -  You're a host of a late-night call show. 
You rarely get actual responses.
Most of the time they are prank calls.
Elise a local girl in the city has been a usual caller for a week.
The gimmick of the day was: "What is your strangest story?" 
Elise called and started describing a very 
familiar murder story that had happened recently 
and that many details haven't been released...what do you do?
